Privacy

Last updated September 2026

Keepsake doesn't have accounts, so there isn't much of you to collect in the first place. Here's exactly what we hold, and why.

No accounts, just a device key

When you post your first keepsake, your browser generates a private token and stores it in your device's local storage. That token is your identity on Keepsake — there's no username, password, or profile. If you clear your browser storage or switch devices without saving an email, we have no way to prove a listing is yours again.

An optional email

You can add an email address when you give something. We store it only to notify you when someone asks for your keepsake and to help you recover access to your dashboard if you lose your device key. We never use it for marketing, and it's never shown publicly.

What a requester shares

If you ask for someone's keepsake, we store the note, email, and optional phone number you provide with that request. This contact information is only shown to the giver, and only once they choose you.

Photos

Photos you upload are stored on Cloudflare R2. We keep a full-size and a thumbnail version of each one for as long as the listing exists, plus a short grace period after it's removed.

Screening

Every listing's text and photos are checked automatically by AI models run by Cloudflare at the moment you post, to keep the marketplace safe. Some listings are also reviewed by a human — just Kiarash, today — before they go live.

Rate limiting

To stop abuse, we keep a short-lived, hashed record of your IP address combined with the date (for example, sha256("2026-09-05:203.0.113.4")). We never store your raw IP address, and the hash can't be reversed back into one.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and doesn't track you across sites. There are no tracking cookies, no ad pixels, and no ads on Keepsake at all.

We don't sell data

We don't sell, rent, or trade any information you give us, to anyone, for any reason.

Deleting your information

You can delete a listing (and its photos) at any time from your dashboard. To remove your email or ask us to delete anything else we hold about you, write to hello@keepsake.ing and we'll take care of it.

Questions

Reach us any time at hello@keepsake.ing.
