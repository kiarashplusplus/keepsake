The fine print, briefly

Why free, why no accounts

It's a gift, not a sale

Keepsake is free because these things aren't meant to be priced. You're not listing an item, you're passing something on to whoever will love it next.

No accounts to maintain

There's no password to lose and no profile to keep up. Your browser holds a private key that proves the listings are yours — that's the whole login. Add an email while you're giving and we can mail you a new key if that browser ever forgets; skip it and clearing your storage means starting over.

Every listing is screened

Photos and stories go through automated screening before they appear at all, and anything borderline waits, unpublished, for a person to look at it. Anyone can report a listing that shouldn't be there.
