The fine print, briefly

Why free, why no accounts

It's a gift, not a sale

Keepsake is free because these things aren't meant to be priced. You're not listing an item, you're passing something on to whoever will love it next.

No accounts to maintain

There's no password to lose and no profile to keep up. Your browser holds a private key that proves the listings are yours — that's the whole login.

Every listing is screened

Photos and stories are checked automatically before they go live, and anyone can report a listing that shouldn't be there.
