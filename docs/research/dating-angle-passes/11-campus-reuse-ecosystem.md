# Campus Reuse & Gifting Ecosystem — Research Findings

**Verdict:** The campus reuse ecosystem is a viable seed/distribution channel, but as an *overlay* on existing institutional and peer infrastructure rather than a green field. There is a well-documented, calendar-anchored behavior of students giving away decor, furniture, and sentimental dorm items for free at predictable national dates (spring move-out, fall move-in), already involving 200+ schools and measurable multi-ton volumes per campus — meaning Keepsake wouldn't need to teach the core behavior, only add the "story" layer on top of it. The single best evidence is the combination of PLAN's ~200-school network (CSMonitor, 2026) and Dropcurb's per-campus tonnage data (Tufts 230 tons, Stanford 69 tons/2024, UNH 80-ton May surplus over baseline) — proof that structured, sentimental-item-inclusive giving already happens at scale on a fixed calendar, which is exactly the pattern a story-first free-gifting app needs to plug into.

## Findings

**Move-out donation programs**
- PLAN (Post-Landfill Action Network), founded 2013 by Alex Freid, has worked with ~200 schools nationwide; founder estimates about half of the US's 2,057 residential colleges/universities now have some donate-and-rehouse program. [verified by fetch, csmonitor.com, 2026] https://www.csmonitor.com/Perspectives/Making-a-difference/2026/0519/college-graduation-trash-furniture-reuse
- Per-campus move-out tonnage (2024 data): Tufts 230 tons/year, Boston University 113 tons (May 2024), Stanford 69 tons (2024)/612 tons cumulative since 2013, UNH 105 tons in May vs. 25 tons/typical month, UT Austin's MoveOutATX 249 tons cumulative. [verified by fetch] https://dropcurb.com/blog/college-move-out-waste-report
- Goodwill "Donate Don't Dump," University of Iowa: 21,158 lbs collected in one move-out window (486 students, 1,036 items); BU's "Goodwill Not Landfill" has diverted 2M+ lbs since 2009, with May 2024 setting a record at 150,000+ lbs. [search snippet only] https://sustainability.uiowa.edu/news/more-21000-pounds-collected-donate-dont-dump , https://www.bu.edu/sustainability/2024/07/18/goodwill-not-landfill-reaches-record-numbers-in-2024
- "Chuck It for Charity" (FSU): 25,852.9 lbs (12.93 tons) in a recent move-out season; program is in its 14th year; UVA has run an equivalent since 1999. [search snippet only] https://sustainablecampus.fsu.edu/get-involved/programs-&-events/chuck-it-charity
- Penn State's "Trash to Treasure" (2002–2019) averaged 60–68 tons/year and raised ~$1M for United Way over 18 years before ending in 2019 (COVID killed a 2020 revival); replaced by a Goodwill partnership. [search snippet only] https://www.statecollege.com/articles/community/penn-state-ends-trash-to-treasure-sale-partners-with-goodwill-for-student-move-out-donations/
- UC Berkeley ReUSE runs year-round donation plus a dedicated "Cooperative Reuse" furniture exchange May 22–31 at move-out. [search snippet only] https://reuse.berkeley.edu/get-involved/donations/
- University of San Diego's "Eco Exit"/Torero Thrift: diverted 4.3 tons in year one; collects at spring move-out, free-distributes to incoming students in fall, explicitly includes decor and household items (not just utilitarian goods), student-led. [verified by fetch] https://www.thecooldown.com/green-business/eco-exit-dorm-program-college-students/
- University of Toronto (Regenesis Toronto) and Toronto Metropolitan University run Free Stores stocked from residence move-out, lost-and-found, and donations — direct Canadian/Toronto evidence. [search snippet only] https://utgsu.ca/services-grants/levy-groups/regenesis-toronto/ , https://www.torontomu.ca/sustainability/zero-waste/reuse-repurposing/furniture-rehome-program/

**Student peer-to-peer channels**
- Fizz marketplace: live on 240 college campuses + 60 high schools; rolled out March–May 2024; 50,000 listings, 150,000 DMs sent; clothing ~25% of listings; sellers average ~2 inquiries before a deal; no payments integrated. [verified by fetch] https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/03/fizz-the-anonymous-gen-z-social-app-adds-a-marketplace-for-college-students/
- Rumie (.edu-verified campus marketplace): 320,000+ students, 10,000+ listings, 1,078 universities, $450k seed round early 2024. [search snippet only] https://magnoliatribune.com/2024/11/17/tanner-mccraney-founder-of-rumie-overcomes-adversity-to-launch-nations-largest-college-marketplace/
- Facebook "Free & For Sale" groups: UC Berkeley's group cited at both ~10,000 and ~47,000 members (sources conflict — treat as "tens of thousands"); Pitt's group has 12,936 members; UCLA/UCSB require @edu verification. [search snippet only, numbers conflict between sources] https://mojo.dailybruin.com/a-buyers-and-sellers-guide-to-navigating-the-ucla-free-for-sale-facebook-page/ , https://pittnews.com/article/129102/news/campus/students-find-furniture-and-more-free-and-for-sale/

**Calendar / abandoned-property evidence**
- Average college student generates 640 lbs of waste/year, concentrated in a 2–3 week May window; ~19 million US college students, ~22% living on campus. [search snippet only, Dropcurb aggregation] https://dropcurb.com/blog/college-move-out-waste-report
- Abandoned-property policies vary sharply: most schools dispose of left items within 24–72 hours with fees of $50–$300; some (Radford) allow 90 days, others (Webber) 21 days. [verified by fetch] https://www.storagescholars.com/blog/can-you-leave-your-stuff-in-your-dorm-after-move-out-day
- University of Washington's SCRAM program reportedly diverted ~30 tons in June 2025 alone via informal student resale ("seasonal arbitrage"); informal networks (group chats, borrowed vans) dominate, not platforms. [search snippet only, could not verify independently] https://www.wokewaves.com/posts/student-resellers-campus-move-out-circular-economy

**Startups**
- Storage Scholars (Wake Forest, 2017): Shark Tank/$250k Mark Cuban investment, now 200+ campuses, ~$10M projected revenue — active, thriving (moving/storage, not gifting). [search snippet only]
- Dorm Room Movers (2007) — active, same category.
- Rumie, Campus Swap, TreasureX (UC Berkeley), Campus Reclaimed (Wake Forest) — all active reuse/resale marketplaces, none story-first.
- Bunz — Canadian barter app (2013, relaunched recently) with a "Student Exchange Booth" feature, general not campus-specific.
- "Dorm Room Movers," "Campus Exchange" as generic terms did not surface a single defunct-named startup matching exactly; no evidence found for a product literally named "Bunz U."

**Senior wills / provenance tradition**
- "Senior wills" are a genuine, century-old tradition of bequeathing items/traits, heavily documented in **high school** newspapers/yearbooks. [verified by search, multiple sources] — **could not verify** this is a widespread *college*-level tradition; Ivy League "Class Day" traditions (ivy planting, orations) are a different, non-object-bequeathing ritual. Treat the "senior wills at college" hypothesis as **not found** at the college level specifically.

## Risks/Objections
- Existing programs are bulk/institutional (weigh-and-sort-and-truck), competing directly for the same donation moment as Goodwill/Habitat ReStore, which have pickup logistics Keepsake lacks.
- Fizz and Rumie already have real network effects in the "sell stuff to another student" space; Keepsake must differentiate on "story-first, free" rather than compete on convenience.
- The move-out window is short (2–3 weeks) and abandoned-property disposal timelines (24–72 hrs at most schools) may be too fast for Keepsake's "post → wait for notes → pick a winner" flow.
- No verified evidence students specifically value provenance/story (the "senior wills" hook is weak at the college level).
- One-person operator scaling across many campus sustainability offices/RA networks is a real coordination burden.

## Campus Reuse Calendar

| Period | What's happening | Keepsake opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–March (spring term) | Baseline low activity; occasional donation drives | Light campus-ambassador seeding, no major push |
| Late April–mid May (spring move-out) | PEAK: Chuck It for Charity, Goodwill Donate Don't Dump, ReUSE Cooperative Reuse, PLAN-network drives; 60–250+ tons/campus | Table/QR code at donation tents; capture "story" at drop-off before items go to bulk sort |
| Late May–August (summer sublets/storage) | Storage Scholars/Dorm Room Movers busy; Fizz/Rumie churn continues | Target study-abroad/sublet niche in college towns; low priority |
| Mid-August (fall move-in) | Free-store redistribution (Torero Thrift, ReUSE giveaway, TMU Furniture Rehome) | Seed leftover May donations as first live listings; mass-onboard incoming students at orientation as requesters |
| Sept–Nov (fall term) | Fizz/Rumie/F&F Facebook steady baseline | Ongoing organic churn, roommate swaps, room refreshes |
| December (winter break) | Lower abandonment (many policies let returning students keep items in place) | Minor secondary donation events at some schools; low priority |
