## Verdict

Across the Houston metro I found **9 verified, named safe-exchange locations** operated by law-enforcement agencies (2 UH/HPD-adjacent, 2 Harris County Constable Pct. 1, 1 Constable Pct. 8, 1 HCSO, 1 Pasadena PD, 1 Pearland PD, plus a Woodlands-area Montgomery Co. Constable Pct. 4 zone whose exact address was not confirmable), and confirmed **HPD itself runs no official program** — it only tolerates station-lot meetups without camera/officer guarantees. The location nearest to **both Rice (77005) and UH (77204)** is the **University of Houston Police Department Safe Trade Location, 4051 Wheeler Ave, Houston, TX 77204** — on the UH campus, about 2 miles from Rice — and it is also the strongest-sourced entry (official .edu page, directly fetched, explicit statement on cameras/hours). The Harris County Constable Precinct 1 zone at 1302 Preston St (downtown) is the next-closest non-campus option (~3–4 miles).

## Findings, items 4–6

- **Non-police alternatives**: No evidence found of METRO transit centers, Houston Public Library branches, or grocery-store (H-E-B) lots being formally designated as police-partnered safe-exchange sites in Houston; H-E-B partnerships with police for this purpose could not be verified anywhere in Texas searches. METRO does staff transit-center security (e.g., TMC Transit Center 24/7 guard) but this is not marketed as a marketplace meetup spot.
- **Buy Nothing norms**: Houston-area Buy Nothing groups follow the same national norm as elsewhere — post-2020, "porch pickup" (leave item outside, no direct contact) is the default handoff method; no Houston-specific written policy document was found, only the general national pattern.
- **Effectiveness data**: No peer-reviewed or police-reported quantitative evaluation of safe-exchange zones' crime-reduction impact was found. The concept traces to Boca Raton PD (2014) and has spread nationally on the untested premise that offenders avoid camera-covered police lots; I found no study measuring before/after crime rates at these sites.
- **National counts**: OfferUp's "Community MeetUp Spots" program has grown to roughly 1,900 locations nationwide (per OfferUp's own 2018 release, since apparently expanded further per later coverage), but no Houston-specific count was retrievable — the searchable directory (safetradestations.com) returned no Houston or Toronto entries when its list page was fetched directly.

## Gaps

Checked with no result: HPD official designated-location program (confirmed none exists — only informal lot use), Harris County Constable Precincts 2/3/6/7, Sugar Land/Missouri City/Katy/League City/Baytown/Cypress-specific municipal PD pages, York Regional Police (Ontario) address list, full Toronto Police Service divisional exchange-zone roster beyond 32 Division, H-E-B/grocery-chain police partnerships, METRO/library-branch designated meetup spots, and any academic crime-reduction evaluation of the safe-exchange-zone concept.
