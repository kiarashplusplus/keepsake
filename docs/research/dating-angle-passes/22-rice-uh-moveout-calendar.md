# Spring 2027 Move-Out / Fall 2027 Move-In Research — Rice, UH, TSU

## Verdict

**Rice:** The realistic ritual window is **~April 23 – May 8, 2027** (last day of classes Apr 23 → study days Apr 24-27 → finals Apr 28-May 4 → commencement May 7-8), with the hard clock being residential-college checkout (appointment-based, $200 fine for skipping it) rather than a published fixed hour deadline — that exact hour rule was **not found** in public text. Strongest source: [Rice Registrar Spring 2027 calendar](https://registrar.rice.edu/calendars/spring-semester-2027) [verified by fetch].

**UH:** The realistic window is bounded by the **Cougar Move-Out donation collection itself, historically April 21 – May 12** (confirmed for 2025; the 2026 window text quotes the identical Apr 21–May 12 dates, so treat 2026 as *unconfirmed — possibly a repeat of 2025 dates, not yet distinguished*), landing just before commencement (**May 12-15, 2027**, confirmed by fetch). UH's actual housing "hard clock" is gentler than assumed: abandoned property in a *room* gets a **30-day claim window**, not 24-72 hours — only *community-area* items are disposed of "at any time." Strongest source: [UH Cougar Move-Out Donation Program page](https://www.uh.edu/sustainability/campus-sustainability/cougar-move-out-donation-program/index.php) [verified by fetch] and [UH Abandoned Property Process page](https://www.uh.edu/housing/current-and-returning-residents/housing-resource-guide/abandoned-property-process.php) [verified by fetch].

**TSU:** Insufficient public detail found — calendar and housing move-out rules not located; **not found**.

## Findings on items 3–6

**3. Donation programs in detail**
- **UH Cougar Move-Out** (2025 window Apr 21–May 12): drop boxes in residence-hall lobbies at **CV1, CV2, Lofts, Cougar Place, The Quad, Moody Towers**. Accepts clothing/coats/shoes/jewelry/purses/hats/toys/blankets/drapes/sleeping bags/backpacks/small kitchen items/books; **refuses appliances, furniture, shelving, floor lamps**. Partners: **Green City Recycler (GCR)** and **Cougar Cupboard** (campus food/essentials pantry) — donations sorted and split between the two. No volumes (lbs/tons) or volunteer/tabling calls found for UH. Contact: James Aguanno, jvaguann@central.uh.edu, UH Student Housing and Residential Life / Office of Sustainability.
- **Rice "Give a Hoot! Donate Your Loot!"**: bins at **all 11 residential colleges**. Accepts clothing/shoes/linens/textiles, household goods/décor; refused-items list **not specified**. Partner: **Green City Recycler**; program run jointly by **Office of Sustainability and Housing & Dining**. Volumes: 4,943 lbs (2023, inaugural year) → 8,922 lbs (2024) → "over 48,000 lbs" cumulative as of July 2026. Won Keep Texas Beautiful's Beautify Texas Award. No dedicated 2025/2026 date range found (only cumulative volume). Adjacent channels for overflow: Rice Career Closet, Dress for Success, Career Gear, Friends of Phyllis Wheatley High School; mini-fridges routed to first-year students via Student Success Initiatives; nonperishable food to the student pantry.
- Sentimental/small-decor items appear implicitly covered under "decor" (Rice) and general categories (UH), but neither program's public text explicitly addresses small sentimental keepsakes — **not found** as a distinct category either way.

**4. Fall move-in reuse/swap events**
- No UH "Coogs Recycle" or campus-wide move-in free-store found; UH does run **Cougar Cupboard** (pantry) and a **Cougar Closet** (professional clothing) and **Coog Cub Closet** (baby items) year-round, not move-in-specific.
- Rice has **EcoReps** (student sustainability reps embedded in all 11 residential colleges) who run **clothing swaps** (leftover items donated to Montrose's Cottage Shop) — not confirmed as tied specifically to O-Week/move-in timing.
- No "Coog Closet" swap program was found; that name did not surface distinctly from Cougar Closet/Cougar Cupboard.

**5. Summer leftovers / redistribution to incoming students**
- **Not found** for either school: no public documentation that move-out donations are stored over summer and redistributed to fall incoming students. Rice's flow (Career Closet, food pantry, mini-fridges to first-years) suggests some redistribution happens but only within the same academic year's new intake context (mini-fridges), not an explicit "hold to fall" pipeline.
