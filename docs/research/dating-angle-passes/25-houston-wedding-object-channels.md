# Houston Wedding-Object Exchange Channels — Research Findings

**Verdict:** No single Houston hub matches Shared Dream Dresses or Buy Nothing Wedding in scale, but three real channels give Keepsake a wedge. (1) **The Giving Gown Foundation**, a Houston charity already running a free-formalwear pipeline (goal: 1,300 girls/year) through a network of Tide Cleaners drop points — the closest existing model to "story-first free matching," just for prom rather than weddings, and a plausible partner or template. (2) **Marry Go Round Bridal** and **Bells and the Ball**, Houston's two named consignment shops, are the physical places dresses already change hands paid — good outreach targets for a free alternative pitch (their consignors who don't sell get a free-donation option) and for flyering. (3) **CultureMap Houston's "This wedding dress has seen 5 brides"** (Mar 2013, Reckling family, displayed at Rienzi/MFAH) is a genuine, verified, Houston-specific heirloom-reuse story — proof local media will cover this angle, and a natural hook for pitching a follow-up story about Keepsake. The single strongest source overall is the **Harris County Clerk's office quote to CultureMap** (~2,500 marriage licenses/month, verified by fetch) — the only hard, sourced local scale number found.

## Other findings

**2. Houston wedding scale**
- Harris County Clerk issues ~2,500 marriage licenses/month → roughly 30,000/year (2022 figure, county clerk quote); no 2025/2026-specific annual total found. [verified by fetch]
- Harris County District Clerk divorce filing statistics by year: **not found** — office has no public per-year statistics page; would require direct contact (832-927-5800 / 201 Caroline Ste 420, Houston).
- Average Houston wedding total cost: cited at **$25,000–$29,000**; Zola 2026 data puts average Houston *venue* cost at $7,291 (range ~$1,300–$45,000+). [search snippet + one fetch-verified page for venue figure]
- Number of Houston bridal shops: not tallied from any single source; multiple consignment/sample shops identified individually above but no comprehensive count found.
- Bridal Extravaganza Show: Jan 30–31, 2027, George R. Brown Convention Center, 220+ vendors, general admission $15 online/$25 door; **vendor booth cost and any nonprofit/free-table policy not published** — requires direct outreach to (281) 340-7777.

**3. "Something borrowed" / cultural reuse culture**
- Vietnamese community: áo dài wedding attire is commonly **rented** (e.g., L'amai Bridal, Houston) rather than owned/passed down — a rental-reuse norm already exists.
- Nigerian community: aso-ebi (matching fabric bought by guests) is the dominant tradition; no dress-lending practice specific to Houston found.
- Indian community: no Houston-specific lending/reuse tradition found. **Not found.**
- No dedicated "bridal closet" lending library (church or cultural) was found in Houston specifically.

**4. Houston wedding media**
- CultureMap Houston has run at least one heirloom-dress feature (2013, verified) and a general Houston thrift-store roundup (2026) but no dedicated "secondhand wedding dress" trend piece was found.
- Houston Wedding Blog (weddingsinhouston.com) ran a heirloom-details wedding feature (June 2025) — repurposed dress fabric, family photo displays — but not full-dress donation/reuse.
- Houstonia Magazine: no matching wedding-secondhand article found in search results.

**5. Divorce side**
- No Houston-specific divorce-dress resale press, divorce-party service, or "what to do with the dress" local coverage was found — all results were national generic advice sites (Life-Saving Divorce, HelloDivorce, etc.), not Houston-datelined.
- NICU Helping Hands' Angel Gown program (turns donated wedding dresses into NICU baby garments) appeared in generic donation-option lists but its Houston-area presence/chapter was **not verified**.

**6. Seasonality**
- Fall and spring (roughly October–April) are cited as Houston's most popular/ideal wedding-weather months, with venues booking 12–18 months ahead for that window. [search-snippet + fetch-verified via Zola's Houston venue guide]
- Local Houston-specific proposal-season data (e.g., holiday/Valentine's spike): **not found**.
- A 2026–27 Houston bridal-show calendar beyond Bridal Extravaganza (Jan 30–31, 2027) was **not found**.
