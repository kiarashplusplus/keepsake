# Keepsake — Seasonal Press-Hook Research

**Verdict:** For a one-person operator, a Valentine's-week "give away what an ex left behind" hook has the best evidence-to-effort ratio. It requires no partnerships, no budget, and no stunt logistics (no live animals, no bonfires, no permits) — just timing a handful of pitches/posts to coincide with a dating hook local TV already treats as an evergreen, low-effort segment. The strongest single proof point is that **Houston's own ABC13 and KPRC/Click2Houston have both run this exact "get rid of your ex's stuff" story before** (Goodwill in 2017, Torchy's Tacos in 2020) — meaning the local press appetite for this precise angle is not hypothetical, it's demonstrated in-market. Zoo/bonfire stunts get more raw press volume but need an institutional partner (a zoo, a restaurant chain) Keepsake doesn't have; the donate/regift angle is copy-paste-able by a solo founder pitching "here's a free, no-cash alternative to donating your ex's stuff — someone local can just come get it."

## Findings

**Valentine's secondhand/anti-consumerist campaigns**
- Goodwill Houston ran "Dump Your Ex's Stuff," a Valentine's donation drive through Feb 14, urging donors to "get rid of the items that keep you stuck in the past" (VP of PR quote), explicitly told to only donate items truly left behind, not things owed back to the ex. [ABC13 Houston, Feb 13, 2017](https://abc13.com/post/goodwill-asks-you-to-dump-your-ex-/1752296/) — [verified by fetch]
- The "Donate Your Ex's Stuff" campaign concept originated with Goodwill Keystone Area (Harrisburg, PA) in 2010 and has since been run by many independent Goodwill regions (Sarasota, Austin, Miami, etc.), each doing its own local PR push. [NBC New York](https://nbcnewyork.com/entertainment/the-scene/Goodwills-Campaign-Helps-You-Forget-Your-Ex-This-Valentines-Day--368610151.html); [KXAN Austin](https://www.kxan.com/news/local/austin/goodwill-central-texas-launches-dump-your-exs-stuff-campaign-for-valentines-day/) — [search snippet only]
- Value Village/Savers runs recurring "Thriftorials" content pegged to Valentine's Day gift ideas and a "reGIFT" theme, but no stated results/metrics were found. [Value Village](https://www.valuevillage.com/thrift-proud/thriftorials/love-these-gift-ideas-for-valentines-day) — [search snippet only]
- No specific viral Buy Nothing Valentine's/ex-regift post was found. [could not verify / not found]

**Stunts that earned press**
- Bronx Zoo "Name-a-Roach": $15 to name a Madagascar hissing cockroach after an ex; **56,000 cockroaches named over the past decade** (per zoo/press framing), returns annually, is national wire-story material every February. [Bronx Times](https://www.bxtimes.com/name-a-roach-2025/) — [verified by fetch]
- El Paso Zoo "Quit Bugging Me": started in El Paso roughly 7 years before its 2025 write-up (~2018), zoo staff describe it spreading to "across the state, across the nation... worldwide," with unsolicited submissions from Normandy and Australia — but no outlet names or engagement numbers were confirmed. [KFOX14](https://kfoxtv.com/news/local/el-paso-zoos-quirky-valentines-event-quit-bugging-me-gains-global-attention) — [verified by fetch, numbers thin]
- Torchy's Tacos "Salty Singles Day" (bring your ex's clothes to burn, get a free "Taco Love" shirt): single-location Austin event (Feb 12, 2020) picked up by **CBS News (national)** and **KPRC/Click2Houston (Houston market)** despite having zero Houston locations involved — direct proof Houston TV news will run a Texas-brand breakup stunt even without local relevance. [Click2Houston](https://www.click2houston.com/news/local/2020/02/08/torchys-tacos-will-torch-your-exs-stuff-at-salty-singles-day-party/) — [verified by fetch]
- Museum of Broken Relationships has run ticketed Valentine's "Fair of the Heart" events (journaling workshops, tarot, photo booth) at its Indianapolis and LA locations, and toured a NYC pop-up ("The EX-hibition" with Bravo). No engagement numbers found. [Downtown Indy](https://downtownindy.org/do/a-fair-of-the-heart-valentines-night-with-the-museum-of-broken-relationships-indianapolis) — [search snippet only]
- Multiple "burn/shred your ex" bar promos exist in different cities each year (Boston, LA, NYC, Tampa) — a recurring, low-cost bar-marketing genre with local press pickup but no cited metrics. [Time Out Boston](https://www.timeout.com/boston/restaurants/galentines-ex-exorcism-at-horse-thieves-tavern) — [search snippet only]

## Risks / objections
- Zoo/restaurant stunts require an institutional partner Keepsake lacks; copying the *format* (not the partner) is what's actually available.
- "Red Tuesday" rests on one dating site's self-reported survey — cite cautiously or not as fact.
- Buy Nothing Project began enforcing its trademark against independent Facebook groups in late 2025, a reminder that "free gifting" branding/legal territory is contested — worth a light trademark check for Keepsake's own name and campaign names.
- Most stunt coverage lacks hard engagement metrics; treat "went viral" claims as press framing, not data.
