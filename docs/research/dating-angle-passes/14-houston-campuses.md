# Houston Campuses Research — Keepsake Launch

## Verdict

**University of Houston is the strongest first campus**, not because it has the richest gifting culture (Rice's does, via Marriage Pact) but because it already has two pieces of infrastructure Keepsake needs pre-built: a **university-run Office of Sustainability move-out donation program** (Cougar Move-Out Donation Program, with named partners and a named staff contact) and a **UHPD "Safe Trade Location"** — a 24/7, camera-monitored police-lobby address explicitly designated for completing online-marketplace handoffs (4051 Wheeler Ave). That second fact is the single best piece of evidence: UH has already institutionalized the exact "in-person handoff of a thing found online" behavior Keepsake depends on, solving the safety objection before Keepsake even launches. UH's scale (48,972 students, largest single Houston campus population) also gives a marketplace the liquidity a school of ~4,800 (Rice) or ~4,300 (UST) may lack. Rice remains the strongest *secondary* target — its dense single-ZIP campus (77005), 70%+ on-campus housing rate, and a Marriage Pact that just had "record-breaking engagement" in its fourth year show real appetite for lightweight matching rituals — but its small size argues for pairing rather than leading with it.

## Risks / Objections

- Several "not found" gaps (TSU, UST, HCU, HCC tabling rules; dating rituals beyond Rice; reuse programs beyond Rice/UH) likely reflect search-index limits, not true absence — a campus visit or direct office outreach would surface more.
- UH's 2027 move-out dates are not yet published; only historical (likely 2025) dates were found, so program timing for spring 2027 must be confirmed directly with James Aguanno's office closer to the date.
- HCC's commuter, multi-campus structure (no dorms, enrollment "shrinking in the city") makes it a poor postcode-radius fit despite its large headcount.
- Rice's tightened 2024 postering/demonstration policy signals rising institutional caution about outside-facing activity, which could complicate any non-student-org promotional presence even where student gifting culture is strongest.
- Derived numbers (UH main-campus enrollment ~43,000; Rice fall-2025 headcount) are estimates from system-wide totals, not officially published campus-only figures, and should be treated as approximate.
